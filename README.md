I study how signals of past climate and environmental change are encoded in the terrestrial sedimentary rock record. My research focuses on terrestrial environments that are particularly sensitive to climatic change, such as wetlands (i.e., palustrine) and lake (i.e., lacustrine) systems. I use a variety of tools including sedimentology, stable isotope and aqueous geochemistry (e.g., carbonate clumped and triple oxygen isotopes), and modern environmental monitoring.
